How to play

You roll five dice and get three rolls per turn.

  • A 6 is your ship. You need it before anything else counts.
  • Once you have a ship, a 5 is your captain.
  • Once you have a captain, a 4 is your crew.
  • Drag a matching die into its slot to claim it. One roll can land all three if you place them in order. Any die left in the tray gets rerolled.
  • With ship, captain and crew aboard, the other two dice are your treasure. Keep it, or use any rolls you have left to reroll. Tap a treasure die to keep it while rerolling the other.
  • Miss any of the three by the end of your third roll and you sink with nothing.

Each round both players take a turn. The bigger haul wins the round, and the first to win three rounds takes the voyage. Who goes first switches every round.
